# Grok agency-builder brief

From the fabri repository or project root, drive the public CLI: inspect bases
with `fabri examples`, scaffold with `fabri new agency <name> --template
<template> --dest <dir>`, serve with `fabri serve --config
<agency>/agent.yaml`, and open the reusable interface with `fabri studio`. Do
not reimplement Fabri scaffolding, serving, or Studio behavior.

Grok: follow [the canonical agency-builder skill](../SKILL.md) for framing, adaptation, and verification.
