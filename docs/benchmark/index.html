Why the number can be trusted

  • An unfinished run earns no score. Whether the work completed and whether it was any good are measured apart, and never quietly averaged together.
  • A change that does nothing spends nothing. Candidate tweaks are bounded and rejected at preflight, so the search can't run up a bill chasing non-differences.
  • Every sub-agent's cost is counted. Static specialists and spawned children alike roll up into one total — the whole company's tree, not just the top.

The honest part

What this establishes

  • Support HQ, as shipped, reliably completes this incident-response workload. Three of three replicas, end to end, from isolated state.
  • It does so at a cost you can quote and reproduce. $0.020200 median per run, counted down the whole tree.

What it does not — yet

  • That memory makes the company better. The memory-vs-control study is the next experiment, not this one.
  • That the result holds for other companies or other tasks. One company, one workload, three replicas. Nothing more is claimed.
  • That the setup is optimal. Exactly one bounded hypothesis has been tried so far.
